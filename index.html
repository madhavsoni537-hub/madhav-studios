Ten years of building things that outlast the trend cycle.

Madhav Studios began as a two-person design desk in Jaipur and grew into a full creative studio working with founders, filmmakers and brands across three continents. We don't believe in one-size templates — every identity, film and interface we ship is built from scratch, shaped by the story it needs to tell.

We're small on purpose. Every project is led by people who actually make the work, not account managers relaying it — which means faster decisions, fewer meetings, and a studio that stays personally invested in what it ships.

How a project moves

The same four stages, every time — refined over a decade of client work.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We start by listening — to your market, your users and where the current approach is falling short.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    Research becomes a point of view: positioning, messaging and a creative direction everyone can align behind.

  3. 03

    Create

    Design, build and production happen in the open, with regular reviews so nothing arrives as a surprise.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We ship, measure what happens, and stay close after launch to refine what the data tells us.
